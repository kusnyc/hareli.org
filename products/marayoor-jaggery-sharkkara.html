Marayoor Jaggery (Sharkkara)

Made from sugarcane grown in the rain-shadow valley of Marayoor without chemical fertiliser, this unrefined jaggery is known for its distinctive golden colour and clean, non-cloying sweetness.

GI status: GI-registered, 2015 Shelf life: 9–12 months in a dry, airtight container Women-led sector
CategoryPreserved & Fermented Foods
RegionKerala — Marayoor, Idukki
GI statusGI-registered, 2015
Typical shelf life9–12 months in a dry, airtight container
Women's role in productionCane-crushing is mechanised, but grading, moulding and packing of the finished jaggery blocks is a major source of income for women in Marayoor's farming cooperatives.

Why this fits a low-wastage SHG model: Solid jaggery blocks, kept dry, last many months without spoiling — a natural fit for a wastage-averse SHG product line.
