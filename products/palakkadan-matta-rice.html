Palakkadan Matta Rice

A red parboiled rice grown in the Palakkad plains, valued for its nutty flavour and firm texture, and a staple of Kerala's everyday and festive cooking.

GI status: GI-registered, 2016 Shelf life: 12+ months dried Women-led sector
CategoryRice, Millets & Pulses
RegionKerala — Palakkad
GI statusGI-registered, 2016
Typical shelf life12+ months dried
Women's role in productionParboiling, sun-drying and hand-pounding of this red rice is traditional women's work in Palakkad's farming households and rice-processing cooperatives.

Why this fits a low-wastage SHG model: Parboiled and fully dried, matta rice is shelf-stable for well over a year.
