Coorg Green Cardamom

Grown on the misty slopes of Kodagu alongside coffee, Coorg cardamom is valued for its fine aroma and is one of South India's signature spice-belt products.

GI status: GI-registered, 2011 Shelf life: 1–2 years, airtight storage Women-led sector
CategorySpices, Aromatics & Dried Foods
RegionKarnataka — Kodagu (Coorg)
GI statusGI-registered, 2011
Typical shelf life1–2 years, airtight storage
Women's role in productionHand-picking and grading on Kodagu's cardamom estates draws heavily on women plantation workers.

Why this fits a low-wastage SHG model: Cured cardamom's long shelf life fits a federation-run spice-aggregation model well.
