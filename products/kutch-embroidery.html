Kutch Embroidery

A family of vividly mirrored and stitched embroidery traditions from the Kutch region, each associated with a different community and instantly recognisable for its dense, colourful needlework.

GI status: GI-registered, 2013 Shelf life: Indefinite Women-led sector
CategoryEmbroidery & Needlecraft
RegionGujarat — Kutch
GI statusGI-registered, 2013
Typical shelf lifeIndefinite
Women's role in productionOne of India's most thoroughly documented women-led craft economies: Kutch's many embroidery styles (Ahir, Rabari, Soof, Mutava and others) are practised almost entirely by women of the region's pastoralist and farming communities, widely organised into women's craft cooperatives.

Why this fits a low-wastage SHG model: A finished textile/accessory product with indefinite shelf life and one of the best-documented women-led craft clusters in India.
