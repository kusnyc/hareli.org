Jharkhand Tussar Silk

A textured, natural-gold wild silk reared on host trees across Jharkhand's forested tribal belt, woven into sarees and fabric for both domestic and export markets.

GI status: GI-registered (as part of regional tussar clusters) Shelf life: Indefinite Women-led sector
CategoryHandloom Textiles
RegionJharkhand — Chaibasa, Dumka belt
GI statusGI-registered (as part of regional tussar clusters)
Typical shelf lifeIndefinite
Women's role in productionCocoon rearing and reeling is organised extensively through Jharkhand's women's self-help groups under state sericulture programmes, making tussar reeling one of the state's largest women-SHG livelihood chains.

Why this fits a low-wastage SHG model: Produced through the same women's SHG networks Hareli already works alongside in Jharkhand, tussar silk is a natural, non-perishable product line for the federation to champion.
