Shital Pati (Cool Mat)

A finely woven, naturally cooling sleeping and floor mat made from split murta cane, traditional to eastern India's hot, humid climate.

GI status: GI-registered, 2019 Shelf life: Indefinite if kept dry Women-led sector
CategoryFibre, Grass & Cane Craft
RegionWest Bengal, Tripura — Cooch Behar
GI statusGI-registered, 2019
Typical shelf lifeIndefinite if kept dry
Women's role in productionSplitting and weaving the murta cane reed into fine, cooling mats is predominantly women's craft work in the Cooch Behar and Tripura weaving clusters.

Why this fits a low-wastage SHG model: A dry natural-fibre product that does not spoil, well suited to holding stock across seasons.
