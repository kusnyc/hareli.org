Chettinad Kottan (Palm-Leaf Basket)

A brightly coloured, tightly woven palmyra-leaf basket traditionally given as part of a Chettiar bride's wedding gifts, now produced as a popular home and gifting product.

GI status: GI-registered, 2012 Shelf life: Indefinite (natural material, does not spoil if kept dry) Women-led sector
CategoryFibre, Grass & Cane Craft
RegionTamil Nadu — Chettinad
GI statusGI-registered, 2012
Typical shelf lifeIndefinite (natural material, does not spoil if kept dry)
Women's role in productionWoven exclusively by women of the Chettinad region from palm leaves in a tradition historically tied to a bride's wedding trousseau, now sustained through women's craft collectives.

Why this fits a low-wastage SHG model: A durable, naturally dry fibre product with effectively indefinite shelf life if kept away from moisture.
