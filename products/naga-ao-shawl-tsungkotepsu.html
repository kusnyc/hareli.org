Naga Ao Shawl (Tsungkotepsu)

A ceremonial shawl of the Ao Naga tribe, densely patterned with motifs of mithun (Naga cattle) heads and human figures denoting social achievement.

GI status: GI-registered, 2020s Shelf life: Indefinite Women-led sector
CategoryHandloom Textiles
RegionNagaland — Ao tribal areas
GI statusGI-registered, 2020s
Typical shelf lifeIndefinite
Women's role in productionWoven by Ao women weavers, traditionally awarded to men who had performed a feast of merit — but the weaving itself is entirely women's work.

Why this fits a low-wastage SHG model: A high-value, non-perishable ceremonial textile suited to planned, demand-matched production.
