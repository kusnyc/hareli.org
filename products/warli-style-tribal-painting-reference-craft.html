Warli-Style Tribal Painting (Reference Craft)

A minimalist white-on-mud-red tribal painting style using simple geometric forms to depict everyday and ceremonial life, traditional to the Warli community of Maharashtra's Palghar and Thane districts.

GI status: Documented tribal art form; formal GI registration not yet confirmed — verify before listing as GI-tagged Shelf life: Indefinite (paper/cloth artwork) Women-led sector

Women's role in productionTraditionally a ritual wall-painting craft of Warli women for weddings and harvest festivals, and increasingly practised by women artists in the contemporary market alongside men from the same Warli families.
