Get involved

Three ways to build this with us.

Capital, capability and scale — the federation needs all three. However your organisation gives, we lead every conversation with women's ownership and community wealth creation, not with machinery.

Partnership

Capital, capability and scale partners.

Capital partners

Philanthropic foundations, CSR programmes and development finance institutions providing grant or blended capital across our three-phase plan.

Capability partners

Development organisations and technical institutions contributing knowledge, systems, training design and institutional expertise.

Scale partners

National missions and ecosystem players who provide reach and replication pathways once the model is proven in its first districts.

We work with strategic philanthropy, bank and corporate CSR foundations, national livelihood missions, and international development and philanthropic partners. Write to us and we will map the right conversation for your organisation.

CSR collaboration

A Schedule VII-aligned engagement, built for multi-year commitment.

Hareli's programme verticals map directly onto women's empowerment, rural development, and livelihood enhancement priorities. We work with corporate CSR teams on scoped, reportable engagements — from a single district pilot to a multi-year, multi-state commitment.

  • Defined outcomes and independent reporting
  • Site visits and direct engagement with women leaders
  • Co-branding options for regional product lines
